# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of private
information. Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and ban
participants whose conduct is inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the FoldLab organization maintainers.
Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible and respond in a fair,
proportionate way.

This summary follows the Contributor Covenant's intent. The project may adopt
the complete current Contributor Covenant text before its first stable release.
